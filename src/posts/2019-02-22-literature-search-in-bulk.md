Hi, 

Normally, when we have a research idea, we begin to search for research paper and try to find related papers through google scholar, pubmed and some databases. This is a lengthy process (especially in google scholar and some databases) since we only can read the title and need to continue flip to next page for another search results. 

By using R, we can easily pull all the title, abstract and other related information in the research paper in bulk.

This is a sharing session @ USM video, where i am sharing how to pull data/research information such as title, abstract and year from Pubmed database, Plos database and Scopus database using R. 

I am very exited to share this to you guys. Thank you for listening for my sharing session.
