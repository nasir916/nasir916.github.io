### The Adventure of Data Analysis

When embarking on the journey of life, whether as new parents, fresh graduates, or newlyweds, the path ahead may seem clear and straightforward. However, the reality is quite different. Life's journey is often windy, confusing, and full of unexpected turns. It is through our experiences that we grow and pave the way for the next great thing to come.

For the author, completing an undergraduate degree in math marked a significant milestone. With a passion for mathematics, the author sought job opportunities in analytics. However, the initial roles in analytics mainly involved basic spreadsheet work, entering numbers and performing simple formulas in Excel. While the people and companies were pleasant, it wasn't the right fit.

A new opportunity arose in higher education, where the author was introduced to the world of business processes. Sitting in meetings, the author gained insights into how students enroll in classes and how enrollment data is collected and presented. Although the work was satisfactory, the author's love for math pushed them to seek something more.

![](/assets/img/e47301_cb76cf4f377d49118b68386d02de683f.png)

### The Beauty of Math

Mathematics, to the author, is a beautiful subject. It can be seen in the intricate patterns found in nature, like the agave plant in Tucson, representing a fractal. Math is also a realm of certainty, where two plus two always equals four. These aspects fascinated the author, and they yearned for a role that allowed them to fully embrace the beauty of math.

During a complete system conversion from a legacy mainframe to a PeopleSoft system, the author's perspective on data analysis shifted. Critical information needed to be accurately reported to state and federal governments, and the new system posed new challenges. Specifically, identifying new first-year students became a priority, and the author seized the opportunity to tackle this task.

### The Importance of People and Data

Understanding data goes beyond spreadsheets and formulas. The author quickly learned that spending time with people and hearing their insights was crucial in comprehending the data process. Face-to-face interactions and knowledge-sharing sessions with colleagues and different departments became invaluable in unraveling the complexities of data analysis.

Working closely with the data itself was the next step in the author's journey. Data analysis is not a perfect science, and errors, quirks, exceptions, and timing differences are prevalent. By immersing themselves in the data, the author gained a deeper understanding of its intricacies and uncovered valuable insights.

### Bias and Mistakes

Everyone brings their biases to the table, and the author was no exception. Whether it's a condiment bias like their son's aversion to mayonnaise or a professional bias, biases can cloud judgment and hinder thorough analysis. The author realized that assumptions based on prior reports or rushing through processes led to mistakes and missed crucial evidence. Over time, they learned the importance of slowing down, asking questions, and embracing a collaborative approach to data analysis.

Making oneself irreplaceable was once the author's primary focus. However, they soon discovered that true value lies in building relationships, sharing knowledge, and fostering collaboration. Rushing through tasks and prioritizing speed over accuracy only led to costly errors and damaged trust.

### A New Perspective: The Power of Story

Transitioning into a new phase of life, the author's perspective on data analysis evolved further. As a part-time analyst and full-time student in a master's program for learning technologies, they discovered the power of storytelling in data analysis. Taking information from the "black box" of data and weaving it into a narrative became the author's passion.

Attending conferences and presenting their projects allowed the author to engage with other professionals and gather valuable insights. They realized that teachers, educators, and instructional designers often lack the necessary information to assess the success of their initiatives. This realization fueled the author's belief that data analysis should be embraced by all, bridging the gap between research and practice.

### The Purpose of Data Analysis

Data analysis is both an art and a science. It requires spending time with people, asking questions, and truly understanding the data process. It uncovers patterns, reveals insights, and enables informed decision-making. The author believes that data analysis has the power to bring people together, creating a space for collaboration, knowledge sharing, and problem-solving.

So, the next time you dive into the world of data analysis, remember to appreciate the beauty of numbers, lift the heavy rocks to uncover hidden insights, and embrace the power of data to strengthen relationships and drive positive change.
