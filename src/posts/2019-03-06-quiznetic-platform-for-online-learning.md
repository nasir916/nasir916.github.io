Salam and Hi, 

In this video, i am showing on how i conduct a tutorial class using a Quiznetic platform. Based on my observation, all my students were very excited with this game platform to be used in their tutorial class. When i asked my students on which tutorial method they like more (either classical method or technological method), they would prefer this kind of method more compared to traditional method. 

I will do a measurement to compare the performance of students grade between traditional methods and technological methods at the end of the semester (later!). 

In quiznetic, students can learn more in less stress and less boring. In teachers perspective, we can see students are giving their fully attention and engagement into the particular subject. 

The input of the questions should be modified so that we can measure the performance. In my view, there are no teachers should say, "my subject is not suitable to online learning". If teachers says their subject is not suitable for online learning, i think that teacher is not ready to get involve into new eras of technology and students. They will also give an astronomical number of excuses to avoid this kind of evolution!. 

"Quiznetic takes gamification to a whole new level. With the style of a traditional board game, this is the ultimate gamified learning experience.  
  
Make a set of questions and build your own themed revision game for students to race each other round whatever kind of track you can imagine. Students join the game on their own devices and have to answer questions to move their player on the game screen. Display the game on a Smart Board at the front of the class so everyone can see where their player is in real-time. Students can answer the questions using laptops, tablets or phones!" - Quiznetic.com
